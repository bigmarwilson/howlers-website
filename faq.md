## Frequently Asked Questions

### What vaccinations are required?
Rabies, Bordetella, and Distemper.

### Are aggressive dogs allowed?
Dogs must be social and non‑aggressive.

### Do humans need to stay?
Yes — Howler’s is a social space for dogs *and* humans.

### What about weather?
Indoor lanes are always open.

### How do you keep the facility clean?
Daily sanitation, turf disinfecting, and staff monitoring.
