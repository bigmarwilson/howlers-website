Frequently Asked Questions
What is Howler’s Backyard?
Howler’s Backyard is Rochester’s first premium indoor dog park and pet‑wellness venue. It’s a 15,000 sq ft flagship designed for year‑round off‑leash play, owner seating, and community programming.
Where will it be located?
The flagship is planned for downtown Rochester, Minnesota — close to Mayo Clinic, new housing, and the city’s growing residential districts.
How is this different from daycare or boarding?
Howler’s is not a drop‑off daycare. It’s a shared experience space where owners stay, relax, work, and socialize while dogs play safely under trained supervision.
Do you offer memberships?
Yes. Memberships provide unlimited monthly access and form the core of the business model. Members also receive perks, event access, and priority scheduling.
Can I visit without a membership?
Absolutely. Day passes are available for single visits and are perfect for trying the park before joining.
How does Howler’s keep dogs safe?
Every dog must have proof of vaccinations and pass a behavior screening. The facility uses controlled entry zones, decompression areas, and trained attendants to maintain safe group play.
How many dogs can be in the park at once?
With 10,000–11,000 sq ft of active play space, Howler’s supports a comfortable daily capacity of 70–90 dogs, with a peak safe limit of roughly 130.
What amenities are available for owners?
Lounge seating, remote‑work tables, flexible event space, and strong Wi‑Fi make it easy to relax or work while your dog plays.
Will there be food or drinks?
Instead of a permanent kitchen or bar, Howler’s partners with licensed food trucks on a rotating schedule for peak hours and special events.
Can I host a private event?
Yes. Birthday parties, adoption events, breed meetups, and corporate gatherings can be booked in designated event areas.
Is Howler’s seeking investors?
Yes. The flagship launch is structured as a seed‑stage raise of approximately $750,000–$1,250,000 to fund buildout, launch marketing, technology, staffing, and operating runway.
What’s the long‑term vision?
To establish a scalable indoor dog park brand that can expand into additional Midwest markets using the Rochester flagship as the operating blueprint.
