Frequently Asked Questions
What is Howlers Backyard?
Howlers Backyard is a neon‑lit, industrial‑inspired outdoor concept designed to transform ordinary yards into immersive hangout spaces with glow, warmth, and atmosphere.
Is this a real build or a concept?
It began as a concept deck — a visual and functional blueprint — but it can be adapted into a real backyard build with the right materials and layout.
Can I customize the lighting colors?
Absolutely. The signature glow uses cyan, green, and subtle pink/orange accents, but the lighting system can be tuned to any palette you prefer.
Does the design work in small yards?
Yes — the zone‑based layout scales up or down. Even compact yards can support fire zones, seating, and glow‑based atmosphere.
