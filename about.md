## Our Story

Howler’s was created to bring dogs and humans together in a space that blends comfort, enrichment, and community.  
We transformed a former feed shop into a modern dog‑friendly social hub with indoor lanes, outdoor turf, and a warm human lounge.

## Our Facility

- Indoor sprint lanes  
- Outdoor K9Grass yard  
- Coffee bar + patio  
- Fire pit seating  
- Staff‑monitored zones  
- Clean, safe, structured environment  

## Our Values

- Safety  
- Cleanliness  
- Community  
- Enrichment
