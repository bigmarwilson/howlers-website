Safety & Capacity

Safety as a Core Operating Discipline

Howler’s Backyard treats safety not as an add‑on, but as a foundational operating requirement. Indoor dog park concepts carry unique sensitivities related to behavior, stress, sanitation, and liability — all of which are addressed through strict protocols and trained supervision.

Behavior & Vaccination Requirements

Every dog must provide proof of vaccinations, pass a behavior screening, and have a member profile with signed waivers and a clear code of conduct. These requirements reduce incident risk and help maintain a predictable, safe environment for all dogs and owners.

Controlled Entry & Transition Zones

The facility uses double‑gate entry, decompression areas, and controlled transition zones to reduce congestion and improve dog handling during check‑in. This design minimizes overstimulation and supports smoother group dynamics.

Staffing for Safety

Trained dog attendants supervise the floor at all times, with increased coverage during peak evening and weekend periods. Staff roles are intentionally separated:

• Floor supervision & dog handling

• Front desk & member services

• Sanitation & cleaning

• Event coordination

Sanitation & Disease Prevention

Strict cleaning schedules, strong ventilation, odor‑control systems, and documented vaccination requirements help prevent illness and maintain customer confidence. High‑visibility sanitation standards reinforce trust and reduce operational risk.

Capacity Planning

With 10,000–11,000 sq ft dedicated to active play, Howler’s supports a safe peak capacity of approximately 100–130 dogs at one time using the industry guideline of 75–100 sq ft per dog.

Day‑to‑day operations target a more comfortable range of 70–90 dogs to maintain strong sightlines, lower stress, and a better customer experience.

Incident Response Protocols

Staff are trained in incident response, separation procedures, first‑aid basics, and communication protocols. Clear signage and member education help reinforce expectations and reduce preventable conflicts.
