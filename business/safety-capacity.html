Safety & Capacity

Safety as a Core Operating Discipline

Howler’s Backyard treats safety not as an add‑on, but as a foundational operating requirement. Indoor dog park concepts carry unique sensitivities related to behavior, stress, sanitation, and liability — all of which are addressed through strict protocols and trained supervision.
