Investment Opportunity

Seed‑Stage Investment

Howler’s Backyard represents a seed‑stage investment opportunity with a target raise of $750,000–$1,250,000. Funds support buildout, flooring, fencing, HVAC upgrades, odor‑control systems, technology, insurance, staffing, and operating runway.

The concept is intentionally designed to minimize capital‑heavy components such as kitchens or bars, reducing buildout risk and accelerating time to launch.

Why Invest in Howler’s?

Rochester’s strong professional density, expanding residential base, and year‑round climate create ideal conditions for a membership‑driven indoor dog park. The market lacks a premium, safe, and social dog recreation venue — positioning Howler’s as a first‑mover with durable demand.

The concept is built for repeat visitation, long‑term retention, and strong community engagement.

Scalable Operating Model

The flagship facility is designed as a blueprint for expansion. Once Rochester stabilizes, additional Midwest markets can be launched using the same operating model, improving valuation and investor returns.

The membership‑led revenue structure creates predictable cash flow and strengthens long‑term unit economics.

Projected Returns

The financial model anticipates break‑even in Year 2 and positive EBITDA in Year 3. As membership density increases, incremental revenue begins to drop through more efficiently, improving profitability and valuation.

Investors benefit from equity appreciation, future financing rounds, and expansion into additional markets.

Use of Funds

• Facility buildout and flooring

• Fencing, gating, and safety infrastructure

• HVAC and odor‑control upgrades

• Technology platforms and member systems

• Staffing and training

• Launch marketing and community programming

• Operating runway for the first 12–18 months

Long‑Term Vision

Howler’s aims to become the Midwest’s leading indoor dog park brand — a scalable, membership‑driven concept built around safety, community, and year‑round recreation. The Rochester flagship is the first step toward a multi‑location regional footprint.
