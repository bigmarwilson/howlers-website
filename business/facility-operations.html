Facility & Operations

Flagship Facility Format

Howler’s Backyard is built around a 15,000 sq ft indoor layout designed to balance dog safety, customer comfort, and operational efficiency. The facility dedicates approximately 10,000–11,000 sq ft to active off‑leash play zones divided by dog size and energy level.

Additional space supports check‑in, vaccination verification, member services, restrooms, storage, cleaning, laundry, staff operations, and owner seating with remote‑work tables.

Space Allocation

10,000–11,000 sq ft: Main off‑leash play areas separated by size and temperament.

1,000–1,500 sq ft: Entry, check‑in, retail, and member services.

1,500–2,000 sq ft: Owner lounge, flexible seating, remote‑work space, and event activation.

1,000–1,500 sq ft: Restrooms, storage, cleaning, laundry, and staff support.
