Financials

Startup Costs

The flagship facility requires an estimated $750,000–$1,250,000 in startup capital. This includes buildout, flooring, fencing, HVAC upgrades, odor‑control systems, lighting, signage, technology, insurance, and initial working capital.

The buildout strategy emphasizes durability, safety, and operational efficiency while avoiding capital‑heavy bar or kitchen installations.

Operating Expenses

Major operating costs include staffing, rent, utilities, insurance, cleaning supplies, technology platforms, marketing, and event programming. Staffing is the largest recurring expense, with coverage increasing during peak hours.

The model is designed to maintain lean overhead while supporting strong customer experience.

Revenue Projections

Year 1 revenue is projected at approximately $457,500, driven primarily by memberships and supported by day passes, events, and programming.

By Year 3, revenue is projected to reach $900,000+ as membership density increases and operational efficiency improves.

Membership Growth

Active membership is projected to grow from 250–325 in Year 1 to 525–650 by Year 3. This growth is supported by Rochester’s expanding residential base, strong pet ownership, and demand for year‑round dog recreation.

EBITDA Outlook

The model approaches break‑even in Year 2 and achieves positive EBITDA in Year 3. As membership density increases, incremental revenue begins to drop through more efficiently, strengthening long‑term unit economics.

Long‑Term Financial Strength

The flagship format is intentionally designed to scale. Once the Rochester location stabilizes, additional Midwest markets can be launched using the same operating blueprint, improving valuation and investor returns.
