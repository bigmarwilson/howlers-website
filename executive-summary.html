Executive Summary

Overview

Howler’s Backyard is a premium indoor dog park and pet‑wellness venue designed to become Rochester’s leading membership‑driven dog recreation brand. The flagship 15,000 sq ft facility blends off‑leash play, owner seating, flexible event space, and community programming into a year‑round experience.

Market Position

Rochester’s healthcare‑driven economy, expanding downtown density, and growing residential base create strong demand for experiential amenities. Howler’s is positioned to serve professionals, families, apartment residents, and visitors seeking safe, convenient, and social dog recreation.

Facility Format

The flagship layout includes 10,000–11,000 sq ft of active play zones divided by size and temperament, along with controlled entry areas, owner lounge seating, remote‑work tables, restrooms, storage, and staff support space. The design prioritizes safety, comfort, and operational efficiency.

Operating Model

Howler’s emphasizes recurring membership revenue supported by day passes, private events, training programs, and rotating food truck partnerships. This approach reduces buildout complexity while maximizing customer dwell time and repeat visitation.

Financial Outlook

The business plan projects strong membership growth over the first three years, with the model approaching break‑even in Year 2 and achieving positive EBITDA in Year 3 as retention and utilization improve. The flagship format is designed to scale into additional Midwest markets.

Investment Case

Howler’s represents a seed‑stage opportunity with a target raise of $750,000–$1,250,000 to fund buildout, launch marketing, technology, staffing, and operating runway. The long‑term value lies in establishing a durable recurring membership base and a replicable operating blueprint.
