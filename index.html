About the concept
From slide deck to real backyard

Why this exists

The Howlers Backyard deck started as a way to imagine a space that feels like a modern industrial venue, but lives at home. It’s about taking the language of steel, concrete, and light—and translating it into warmth, comfort, and everyday use.

The website mirrors that same energy: deep charcoal, multi‑color neon, and clear, simple storytelling that feels like a well‑built presentation instead of a cluttered brochure.

Core ideas

Every decision in the deck points to three things: experience, flow, and vibe. This site keeps those front and center so anyone landing here understands the “why” before the “what.”

Backyard experience
How a night at Howlers feels

Zones & flow

The deck breaks the backyard into intentional zones: entry, fire, bar, seating, and movement. Each zone is lit, furnished, and arranged to keep people circulating without losing the feeling of one shared space.

Lighting is layered: ambient glow, focused accents, and practical fixtures that keep the space usable without killing the mood.

Lighting & materials

Steel, concrete, and dark stain carry the industrial backbone. Neon, warm bulbs, and fire bring the human side. The multi‑color glow—cyan, green, with hints of pink and orange—adds motion without turning the yard into a theme park.
