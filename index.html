About the concept
From slide deck to real indoor dog park

Why this exists

Howler's is built for Rochester’s professionals, families, and apartment residents who need a safe, year‑round place for dogs to exercise and socialize. The city’s healthcare‑driven growth, downtown density, and visitor economy make a membership‑based indoor dog park a natural fit.

Instead of traditional daycare or boarding, Howler's focuses on owner‑and‑dog experiences: off‑leash play, seating and work space, and community programming that keeps people coming back.

Core ideas

The business model is intentionally simple: recurring memberships as the anchor, supported by day passes, private events, training‑style programming, and food truck partnerships. That structure reduces buildout complexity while still delivering a strong hospitality experience.

Membership‑led revenue 15,000 sq ft flagship format Safety and supervision first Food trucks, not full kitchen

Concept snapshot

  • Indoor dog park and pet‑wellness venue built around recurring memberships.
  • 15,000 sq ft flagship format with separate play zones and owner seating.
  • Designed for year‑round dog exercise, social connection, and community events.

Design pillars

  • Membership‑led model with day passes, events, and programming as add‑ons.
  • Safety‑first layout: controlled entry, decompression zones, and trained floor staff.
  • Food truck partnerships instead of capital‑heavy bar or kitchen operations.
